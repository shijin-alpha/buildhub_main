## 💡 What Makes This Real Data?

### The ML predictions are based on:

1. **Actual Spending Patterns**
   - Stage payment requests (paid/approved)
   - Custom payment requests (paid/approved)
   - Total amount spent vs budget

2. **Progress Tracking**
   - Completion percentage
   - Progress updates count
   - Days elapsed since project start

3. **Risk Calculation Logic**
   - **Cost Risk:** Spending efficiency (spent/progress ratio)
     - High: Spending > 150% of progress
     - Medium: Spending 120-150% of progress
     - Low: Spending < 120% of progress
   
   - **Time Risk:** Schedule adherence
     - High: Progress < Expected - 30%
     - Medium: Progress < Expected - 15%
     - Low: On track or ahead

4. **Completed Project Analysis**
   - Actual final costs vs original estimate
   - Actual completion time vs planned time
   - Performance rating based on outcomes
